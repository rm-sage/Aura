Aura 2.4.1

## Improvements
- Posters and art fade in gently as they load, with the loading placeholder fading out beneath them, on every page that shows titles: Home, Search, Library, Queue, Airing, History, Discover, Calendar, Live TV and catalog pages. Images already loaded fade in too, so moving between pages is no longer abrupt.
- The loading placeholder's shimmer is tinted to suit each theme, and is darker on dark themes such as Haze and Midnight so it no longer glows against the page.
- The lines joining Queue and Airing to Library in the rail are redrawn. Opening one of those pages sends a glint of light along its branch with the same spring motion as the rest of the glass, and the branch stays lit while you are there. With reduced motion on, the branches simply crossfade.
- When you skipped one or more versions, the update popup now shows the notes for each of them, newest first, under the new version's notes.

## Fixes
- Faint square corners no longer show beside the Home search bar while slowly scrolling past the banner.
- Searching no longer leaves rectangles behind where the search bar was when the results page opens.
- The rail no longer highlights Home while search results are shown.
- On Home, the banner and rows now scroll right up to the title bar instead of vanishing just below it, which left a thin strip under the title bar.
- The watched, in progress, planned and skipped marks are centred in their circles (they sat about a pixel right and down).
