Aura 2.4.0

Aura 2.4.0 gives the whole app a new glass look. Navigation is now one floating rail, you can pick a Material style (Matte or Clear) with a Cursor glow, and you can give any theme your own accent colour. Notifications, Home, the detail page and the player are redesigned. Anime opening and ending detection works again after AnimeThemes changed its service, AIOMetadata running inside AIOStreams is now supported, search results appear as each catalog answers, and first-run setup has been rebuilt. Trakt support has been removed: scrobbling now goes to AniList and Simkl.

## A new look
- Navigation is one floating glass rail on the left: Notifications at the top, then your profile and Watch Party, then the pages. A highlight slides between rows as you move over them, and on short windows the page list scrolls inside the rail.
- Settings > Theme > Material style sets how menus, panels and controls look. Matte blurs what is behind it under a dark tint. Clear stays see-through and bends the light at its edges; it needs hardware acceleration and draws as Matte without it. New installs start on Clear, existing installs keep Matte, and Notifications points out the new options once.
- Cursor glow: glass near the pointer catches a glint along the edge facing it, and page titles brighten near it. Edge highlights, Surface glow and Glow size sliders sit under it. Not shown on the High Contrast themes.
- Accent colour: give any theme your own accent colour in Settings > Theme, from a colour picker, a hex code or suggested colours. Each theme remembers its own, it syncs to your other devices, and Aura warns you when a colour is hard to read on that theme.
- Every switch and segmented control has one new design that catches the Cursor glow. Segmented controls also work with the arrow keys.
- Every page, menu, dialog and toast uses the same glass.
- Your profile row and profile card show your initials.
- Watch Together is now called Watch Party.

## Home and cards
- The Home banner has rounded corners, and the colours of its art glow softly into the page around it on every theme. Row titles pick up that colour.
- The card under the pointer casts a soft glow in its art's colours.
- Badges and progress bars on posters are frosted glass. Watched, in progress, planned and skipped marks are one larger size on every page, and in progress is a half-filled ring.
- A series' progress bar on Continue Watching is coloured like Your Progress on the detail page: watched green, skipped purple, in progress amber, aired white and not yet aired faint.
- On posters with rating chips at the bottom, the progress bar now sits above the chips.
- Right-click any title you have started for Reset Progress. It explains what will change and asks first.
- Continue Watching countdowns stay on one line, and are left off cards too small to fit one.
- "View all" is a small button beside each row title.

## Notifications
- Notifications open as a side sheet beside the rail, grouped into Today, Yesterday, This week and Earlier, with posters and a dot on new items.
- A new notification grows out of the Notifications row as a small bubble, joined to it by a line of light, for 5 seconds. Further arrivals merge into it with a count, and clicking it opens the sheet at that item.
- In the sheet, the arrow keys move between items, Enter opens one, Delete dismisses it and Escape closes the sheet.
- A message that repeats shows once with a count instead of stacking up.

## Detail page and player
- The detail page's info and episode panels are glass. Its tabs sit in one bar that wraps on a narrow window, and one highlight slides along the episode and stream lists.
- Player menus, the control bar and dialogs share the rail's look and read more clearly over bright video.
- The exit button is now a back arrow (hover for "Exit playback" and the Esc shortcut). The title and episode info sit on the video without a box, and the skip prompt and Next-Up card line up under the back button.
- Subtitles move up only when the player controls would cover them, and only as far as needed.
- The loading screen's logo and title are larger and scale with the window.

## Opening and ending skips
- Some episodes play the opening song over the end credits instead of an ending of their own, or the ending song at the start. Aura now recognises the song from the other end and offers to skip it, labelled "Theme match (song from the other end)", and names the song when the show has only one of that kind.
- These matches are rated Risky, since a final episode can play its opening song over the epilogue: they always ask first, and at the end of an episode the skip prompt and Next-Up card show only with the player controls.

## AIOStreams and AIOMetadata
- AIOMetadata running inside AIOStreams is now supported alongside installing it on its own. First-run setup shows it as already installed.
- Catalogs you hid from Home in AIOMetadata stay off Home when it runs inside AIOStreams (they are still in Discover). Catalogs whose only filter choice is "None" are kept off Home too.

## Search
- An addon's search catalogs are searched up to 12 at a time instead of one after another. Each row appears as soon as its catalog answers, in the usual order, and catalogs with no results close up.
- Catalogs still searching hold their place with a one-line placeholder, so rows barely move as results arrive.
- A catalog that doesn't answer (an addon is down or rate limited) shows "did not answer" with a Retry button that asks just that catalog again, instead of looking like it found nothing.

## Home banner logos
- The Home banner shows each title's logo art from whichever addon provides your metadata, Cinemeta included. Before, this needed AIOMetadata installed on its own.

## First-run setup
- Setup has been rebuilt in five steps: Welcome, Look, Playback, Addons and Tracking. It matches the rest of the app and fits the smallest window size.
- Welcome offers Sign in for guests and asks the crash report question (no longer a separate popup, and never over the sign-in screen). Importing a settings file brings over everything an export holds.
- Look lets you try the themes and the Material style.
- Playback covers audio and subtitle language, avoiding dubs, HDR, opening and ending skips, autoplay and whether closing keeps Aura in the tray. Every choice saves as you make it.
- Addons has corrected descriptions, and Cinemeta and OpenSubtitles install in one click.
- Tracking connects AniList and Simkl right in setup.
- Settings > Backup & Restore has a "Run setup again" button, and "Suggested addons" on the Addons page opens just the addon list.

## Trakt removed
- Aura no longer sends watched movies and episodes to Trakt. Scrobbling goes to AniList (anime) and Simkl (movies, series and anime).
- The Trakt row in Settings, History's Scrobble to Trakt, "Open in Trakt" and the Trakt score are gone.
- Saved Trakt sign-ins are deleted from Windows Credential Manager automatically. Your plays on Trakt are not touched; you can remove Aura's access in Trakt's own settings.

## Settings
- HDR mode explains its choices in plain words: "Convert HDR to fit this screen" (tone-mapping) and "Send HDR to my HDR display" (passthrough). A Recommended tag marks the one that suits your display, and tags show whether Windows reports HDR support and whether HDR is on.
- HDR mode is now set per computer and no longer syncs or travels in a settings export. A new computer starts on the recommended choice, and if Windows has HDR on while Aura is set to Convert, Notifications asks once whether to switch.
- HDR Off is gone: it moves to Convert, which looks the same for normal videos and keeps the bright parts of HDR videos instead of clipping them to flat white.
- Subtitle language has an Off choice, in Settings and in setup. You can still turn subtitles on from the player's Subtitles menu.
- Screen readers now read out toasts, and every Settings switch, dropdown and slider has a name.
- The Debug Panel under Settings > About has been removed. The Performance Monitor still shows frame drops.

## Performance
- Playing a video takes much less work: only the player's controls and the Next-Up card now follow each position update, where the whole app used to redraw about 30 times a second. Moving the mouse over the player no longer redraws its controls either.
- The moving colours in the player's progress bar no longer repaint the bar on every display frame.
- Dragging a slider in Settings saves once when you let go, instead of on every step of the drag.
- Slow decorative animations (the sign-in logo, slider shimmers, the buffering title, the Watch Party status dot) redraw about 30 times a second instead of on every display frame.
- The same poster requested twice at once is resized once.

## Fixes
- Changing the audio language in first-run setup replaced Aura's audio order ("original, en") with that one language, so anime played English dubs and foreign films lost their original audio. Aura repairs this once per account after updating and tells you in Notifications, with an Undo button if you had picked that single language on purpose.
- The subtitle language and anime audio and subtitle choices in first-run setup were never saved.
- AnimeThemes retired the service Aura used for theme songs, so since 8 November many shows got no theme match. Theme matching works again.
- A stream picked a while before playing (a Next-Up pick, a detail page left open in the tray, an unanswered resume prompt) could stay on Loading with no picture. A stream link more than 2 hours old is now refreshed first, with "Refreshing link" on the stream row, and a detail page left open while Aura was hidden reloads its stream list when you come back.
- A stream whose video never decoded left the player on Loading forever. Aura now spots it after a few seconds and retries with a fresh link.
- Retrying a failed stream could switch to a torrent-only copy that can't play. It now keeps to copies with a playable link, on the same debrid service when it can.
- Choosing Send HDR, or changing its peak brightness, during a normal (SDR) video left that video dim and flat until the next one.
- With subtitles off for a title, a default subtitle track could flash on at the start, and a subtitle loaded from the OpenSubtitles search stayed hidden.
- The loading screen could show the plain title or another title's logo, and wide logos came out very small.
- With AIOStreams providing your metadata, the Calendar could leave out most of your shows, and quick repeated searches could come back empty, because of its rate limit. Both now pace themselves to it.
- When an addon inside AIOStreams failed, a fake "[X] addon name" title could appear on the detail page, in hover cards and as tiles in rows and search results. A row made only of these now shows Retry and its last good items.
- Removing a title from Continue Watching could bring back a title you had removed on another device, and if the change couldn't be saved the card vanished and came back later without a word. Aura now checks your account first and, on failure, puts the card back straight away with a message.
- The Home banner could flick through several titles while Home loaded, or when you came back to it. It now opens straight on its first title, comes back on the title you left it on, and no longer changes titles behind a detail page or a video.
- With the Watch Party panel open, the next click only closed it and hovering did nothing. A click outside it now closes it and still lands on what you clicked, and hovering works while it is open.
- In a confirmation dialog, Enter always confirmed, even with Cancel selected. It now presses the selected button.
- Smooth wheel scrolling stopped abruptly at the end of each scroll, which could make the page seem to jiggle. It now slows to a stop.
- The Calendar's popout for busy days was cut off at the top of the page and could scroll sideways out of reach. It now fits the window and shows each episode number.
- Release ages in stream chips could show long decimals such as "16.89044138888889h".
- Continue Watching art from an AIOMetadata install with a custom name and address wasn't found.
- Reconnecting AniList from an expired sign-in notification gave no message when the sign-in could not start.
- Pinning a different hero catalog while Home was open kept showing the old catalog's slides, sometimes labelled with the new catalog's name.
- After switching Stremio accounts, the profile popup could still show the previous account's email, account id and Premium date.
- Picking a different episode from the Episodes panel on the end screen opened the sources for the episode that had just finished.
- After removing a title from Continue Watching and then watching it again, it could stay out of Continue Watching with no Resume for a few minutes.
- With the metadata addon offline or rate limited, shows could disappear from the Calendar until a later reload.
- After voting on a skip segment while paused, the vote arrows could stay greyed out on "Cooling down".
- If the connection dropped while downloading the playback engine or ffmpeg, the download stayed stuck at the same percentage with no error. It now fails and can be retried.
- Downloading ffmpeg from Settings while a skip scan was already downloading it made both fail.
- Screen readers can now reach a notification's own buttons (Dismiss, Undo, Reconnect) separately.
- Snapping Aura into a narrow Windows 11 snap layout could make the window smaller than it can be dragged to. It now keeps its minimum size.
- When a finished show gets a new episode, Aura keeps it out of Continue Watching until you come back to it. That list was shared by every account on a computer, so one person's show could disappear from another account's Continue Watching. It is now kept per account, and coming back to a show on one device now brings it back to Continue Watching on your other devices too.
