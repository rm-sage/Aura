Aura 2.2.1

Aura 2.2.1 makes restoring the window smooth again.

## Fixes
- Restoring Aura from the taskbar or the tray showed a small box flying to the corner of the screen before the window appeared. The window now grows back into place with its content, like other apps.
- Restoring from the tray could take about a second; it is now immediate.
