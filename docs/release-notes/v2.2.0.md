Aura 2.2

Aura 2.2 adds Simkl scrobbling alongside Trakt and AniList, a new skip-time source in IntroDB, manga chapter info for anime, and a much simpler first run: your Stremio addon order now decides which addon does what, just like the official apps.

## Simkl
- Connect Simkl in Settings > Scrobbling, next to Trakt and AniList. Sign-in opens in your browser and stays connected; Aura renews it quietly.
- Finished movies and episodes are added to your Simkl history automatically, anime included (it goes to AniList and Simkl both).
- The History page gets a Simkl button on each row, and "Scrobble all" sends your Simkl rows in one batch.
- If Trakt, AniList or Simkl ever stops accepting Aura's sign-in, a notice now tells you so, with a Reconnect button, instead of scrobbles quietly stopping.

## IntroDB skip times
- Openings, recaps and endings now also come from IntroDB, a community database that covers many shows AniSkip does not, including non-anime TV.
- Well-supported IntroDB times rank alongside AniSkip; times from a single submission only ever offer a skip prompt.
- The skip menu now shows where each skip time came from.
- Add a personal IntroDB API key in Settings > API Keys and the skip times you submit are shared with IntroDB too.

## Manga chapters
- Anime detail pages list where the anime has reached in the manga, and which chapter to continue from, under Details.
- Story arcs and seasons show the chapters they cover.
- Hover an episode's number to see the chapters it adapts, and the player shows them next to the episode title, for shows whose fan wiki lists them. Anime-original episodes say so.

## Addons work like Stremio
- The addon order in your Stremio account now decides which addon provides metadata, search, subtitles and Home rows. Most people no longer need to pick providers at all.
- Home now shows every catalog of every addon you have installed, loading rows as you scroll.
- The per-job provider pickers and other power-user settings now live behind "Show advanced settings", under the Settings search box. Searching still finds them.
- Refreshing an addon while signed in now saves its updated catalogs to your Stremio account, so they survive a restart and reach the official apps. Aura only saves when it can do so safely, and tells you when it did not.
- Addons that list their own streams for an episode are honoured, as in Stremio.
- Subtitle addons now receive the file name and file hash, so the best-matching subtitles come first.

## Detail pages
- Right-click the backdrop on a title's page and choose Change backdrop to pick any artwork Aura has for it. The choice is remembered.
- Arcs view now includes episodes newer than the arc list, grouped as "Newer episodes".
- Episode rows show one number; hover it to see the overall episode number where the two differ.

## Other improvements
- The History page's selection actions now live in its header, where they are hard to miss.
- Scrobble All, and scrobbling a selection, can send to just one service: use the arrow beside the button.
- Watch Trailer shows progress straight away.
- Settings > Optional Components notes when a newer yt-dlp exists, in case trailers stop working.

## Fixes
- Trailers failed to play; they play again, and a failure now explains itself.
- Reloading Aura during playback kept the video playing behind the page; a reload now ends playback, and your resume position is kept.
- Reset all settings did not reset Aura's own settings.
- A title's backdrop could change between opening it normally and after a reload.
- Arc artwork could show the wrong arc's image, and did not appear after a reload.
- Some metadata addons listed hundreds of future episodes as if they had aired; those placeholders are now hidden.
- Some One Piece and Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War episodes never reached Trakt.
- An AniList sign-in approved after two minutes could be saved to the wrong account.
- Reordering addons could remove an addon listed twice from your Stremio account.
- An expired Stremio session was not always recognised, leaving addon changes stuck.
- Automatic skip detection now updates FFmpeg when a newer version is required.
- On a file without built-in subtitles, the automatically chosen subtitle could be a poorer match.
- Settings was cramped at the smallest window size.
