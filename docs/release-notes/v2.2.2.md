Aura 2.2.2

Aura 2.2.2 finds openings and endings by listening for the show's own theme songs, tells you how sure it is about every skip, and makes the player's menus much easier to read.

## Smarter opening and ending skips
- For anime, Aura now finds the opening and ending by matching the show's theme songs in the episode's own audio. Timings from AniSkip and other sources that disagree with the song are set aside.
- Endings stop where the song stops, so after-credits scenes play. A next-episode preview gets its own Skip Preview prompt and never skips by itself.
- An opening that starts the episode now offers its Skip prompt a few seconds into the song.
- Autoplay waits for the end of the episode instead of counting down during the ending, so after-credits scenes and previews always play.
- Anything Aura is unsure about asks first, even on Auto, and the prompt says why.
- Hovering a skip marker on the timeline shows where its timing came from.

## Skip confidence
- Every skip prompt now shows how sure Aura is: Verified (found in this episode itself), Likely (usually right, not confirmed) or Risky (may cut into the story), with a one-line reason.
- The same badge appears on the timeline hover card, in the AuraSkip menu and on the notice after an automatic skip. Settings > Anime OP / ED Skip explains the three levels.
- Risky skips always ask first, even on Auto. An ending chapter that runs to the very end of the file now asks first too, since it can hold a scene after the credits.

## Player
- The AuraSkip menu is now a centred panel like Switch source, with larger, clearer text.
- The speed, audio, subtitle, upscaler, subtitle style and More menus share one cleaner look with larger, brighter text, and so do the subtitle search, Live Sync, Watch Together and episode panels.
- Hovering the volume button slides a volume bar out to its left that you can drag right in the control bar. Clicking the button still mutes.

## Fixes
- Turning Hardware acceleration off in Settings had no effect. It now applies after a restart.
- Aura kept using a noticeable amount of CPU while hidden in the tray. It now sits almost idle there.
- On later seasons of some anime (Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War among them), skip markers could be tens of seconds off because they came from the first season's episode.
- The Anime4K modes menu could open partly off the screen.
